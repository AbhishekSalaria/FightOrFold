01 · Summary

The idea in three lines

What

An AI agent that works through a merchant's chargeback queue and makes the call on every case.

For whom

Finance ops leads at online merchants who handle chargebacks against deadlines.

Why it's different

It weighs cost, not just the chance of winning, and shows the math behind every decision.

02 · The problem

Every chargeback is a small bet

When a customer asks their bank to reverse a payment, the merchant gets a chargeback. Each one has a deadline, an amount at stake, and proof scattered across orders, delivery records, support emails and device data.

The merchant pays a fee even when it wins, so fighting a small chargeback can cost more than simply accepting it. As a result, merchants often fight cases they will lose and give up on cases they could win.

03 · What the agent does

From queue to decision

  1. 1

    Reads the list of chargebacks

    Amount, reason, stage and deadline for each one, from Airwallex.

  2. 2

    Collects the proof

    Device and IP match with earlier orders, delivery signature, refund history and support emails. It also points out missing proof, such as "no delivery signature" or "support never replied". Missing proof can change the decision.

  3. 3

    Does the math

    Compares the amount at stake with the fee, staff time, deadline and an estimated chance of winning, clearly labelled as an estimate.

  4. 4

    Decides and acts

    ChallengeUploads the proof and sends a response that the AI model writes from the facts.
    AcceptAccepts and refunds the customer in one step, then checks that exactly one refund was made.
    EscalateSends the case to a person with the amount, reason and proof on one screen.
  5. 5

    Decides again when the bank pushes back

    If the bank rejects the proof, the agent works out whether fighting again is still worth the cost, or stops.

04 · How it decides

Two numbers, one comparison

Minimum chance needed=extra cost of fighting÷amount at stake

The minimum chance comes from the merchant's own fees. The estimated chance comes from an evidence checklist for each chargeback reason. The agent compares the two.

Challenge

Proof clearly clears the bar

Estimated chance is well above the minimum needed.

Accept

Fighting can't pay off

The cost is higher than the amount, or the proof is too weak.

Escalate

Too close to call

The numbers are close, the proof conflicts, or the merchant may be at fault.

Escalate

Then the twist: the bank rejects the proof

  1. RFI · REQUIRES_RESPONSEThe $480 chargeback arrives with a due date.
  2. RFI · CHALLENGEDThe agent uploads proof and challenges.
  3. Bank rejects the proofThe issuer escalates the case.
  4. CHARGEBACK · REQUIRES_RESPONSEThe agent recalculates, counting only the cost of fighting from here on.
  5. WON or LOSTThe case ends with a final Airwallex status.
Fight again

$480 case: the first $35 is already spent. Another $35 against $480 needs only 7%, and the weakened proof still clears it.

Stop and accept

If the same thing happened on a $40 case, $35 ÷ $40 would need 88%, which weakened proof can't reach.

07 · What makes it different

It knows when not to fight

Thinks about cost, not just winning

It will sometimes say "give this one up", and it shows why.

Missing proof counts

An unanswered support email can change "fight" into "accept" or "ask a person".

Recalculates when things change

Costs differ at each stage, and the agent decides again when the bank responds.

Adds to Airwallex's AI Dispute Automation

That feature suggests challenge or accept. FightOrFold adds the cost math, gaps in the merchant's own proof, and the second round.

08 · Architecture

Code handles the money, the AI model reads the evidence

Amounts, fees, limits and dispute states live in code. The AI model only reads emails and documents, matches proof to each chargeback reason, and drafts responses through a fixed set of tools.

Safety rules built into the code

  • One action per chargeback at each stageA dispute can't be accepted and challenged at the same stage.
  • Never refunds twiceAccepting refunds in the same call; every action carries a unique ID so it can't repeat by mistake.
  • A person approves every escalationThe approval shows the amount, reason and proof being approved.
  • Everything is loggedEvery decision is saved with its proof and numbers. Sandbox test calls stay separate from the decision logic.

10 · Measuring success

How we'll know it works

We measure net recovery (money recovered after fees and staff time) compared with fighting every chargeback, not just win rate.

In the demo, a batch of 20 to 30 sample disputes runs with a live counter comparing FightOrFold, "fight everything" and "accept everything".

Assumptions: sandbox only; merchant proof is sample data; fees are set in the app because the sandbox doesn't charge them; win chances are estimates.
